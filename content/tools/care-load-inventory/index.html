Care load inventory

Caring for a parent while working is hard to talk about when nobody can see the whole load. List every task, roughly how long it takes and who does it. You get the total, what could come off your plate, and a printable list to bring to a family conversation.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; your answers are saved only on this device.

Tick everything that happens in a normal week

Include the invisible work: calls, chasing, worrying on call. Then set rough hours and who does it.

Tick a task and add hours to see your care load.

This is a planning list, not medical, legal or financial advice. For decisions about your parent's health, safety or money, a doctor, a social worker or care manager, or your local agency on aging can help. In the US, the Eldercare Locator (1-800-677-1116, eldercare.acl.gov) can point you to local services. Looking after yourself counts too: if you are running on empty, tell your own doctor.
