Most couples have one fight that keeps coming back in nearly the same words. Answer a few prompts about yours and get a one-page plan for one calm conversation about it. Nobody gets blamed: the loop is the problem you solve together.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; your answers are saved only on this device.
