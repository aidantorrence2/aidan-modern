Hook (first line on screen and in the caption):

Script:
Open on an airport arrivals board. VO: 'New city, thirty shoots already booked.' Cut to the sign-up page on a phone: dates, moodboard picker, WhatsApp field. VO: 'One page per city. They pick a theme, drop a photo, book a slot in their time zone.' Cut to a list of sign-ups filling. End card: 'City Pages. $19 a month. Pre-launch, link in bio.'

Keep running: some sign-ups under threshold; try a second hook or segment for 72h.
Kill: 20+ pitches and a post with zero sign-ups, or 7 days with under 3 sign-ups.
