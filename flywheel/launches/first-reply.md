## 15–30 second post

Hook (first line on screen and in the caption):
> The inquiry came in at 2pm during a wedding. I replied at 11pm. She'd booked someone else at 4.

Script:
Open on a phone face-down on a reception table, a notification glowing. VO: 'Inquiry at two. Replied at eleven.' Cut to a reply going out at 2:02: 'Yes, 14 June is open', a pricing guide attached, a call-slot link. Cut to the photographer's phone: the lead, the reply already sent, a 'add a personal line' tap. Cut to a board: replied, call booked, proposal, booked. VO: 'Date checked, guide attached, call offered. Two minutes, in your voice.' End card: 'First Reply. $6 a month. Pre-launch, link in bio.'

Caption:

## Kill / build check (72h after the first traffic)

Build now: 1 pre-order, or 3 "I'd pay" clicks, or 10 sign-ups.
Keep running: some sign-ups under threshold; try a second hook or segment for 72h.
Kill: 20+ pitches and a post with zero sign-ups, or 7 days with under 3 sign-ups.
