Price: $5/month (unlimited clients and links)
Buyer: family, newborn, senior and wedding photographers whose bookings mostly come from word of mouth and who track thank-you credits by memory

## 15–30 second post

Hook (first line on screen and in the caption):
> Half my bookings come from past clients. I track their thank-you credits in my head, which means I forget half of them.

Script:
Open on a notes app: 'Sarah sent 2? Owe her a print? Check.' VO: 'Half my bookings are referrals. I track the credits in my head.' Cut to a phone: a referral card with a client's name and '$50 off for every friend who books'. A friend books; the referrer's name appears on the booking; a credit shows on both sides. Cut to a tree of names, top senders at the top. VO: 'Every client gets a link. Every booking knows who sent it. Every credit is counted.' End card: 'Referral Card. $5 a month. Pre-launch, link in bio.'

Caption:
A personal referral link for every client, with real credits tracked on both sides. $5/month. Not built yet; tell me if you'd use it. aidantorrence.com/offers/referral-card
