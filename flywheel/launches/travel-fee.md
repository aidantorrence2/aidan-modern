## 15–30 second post

Hook (first line on screen and in the caption):
> Quoted $75 travel from memory. Drove 140 miles each way, hit a closure, paid for a hotel. The $75 covered the parking.

Script:
Open on an inquiry reply being typed: 'travel is around $75'. VO: 'Around. From memory.' Cut to the day: a motorway closure, a late hotel booking. Cut to a phone: a venue address pasted, round-trip miles, drive time, fee and a hotel line appear, worded for the quote. VO: 'Your rates, their address, the real number.' End card: 'Travel Fee. $3 a month. Pre-launch, link in bio.'
