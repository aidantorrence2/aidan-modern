## 15–30 second post

Hook (first line on screen and in the caption):
> Posted the sneak peek. Tagged seven vendors. The florist texted: 'you forgot me.' Third time this season.

Script:
Open on a caption being typed: @venue @planner @florist... a pause, a scroll through old messages for a handle. VO: 'Seven vendors, every post, from memory.' Cut to a phone: a wedding's vendor list, one tap copies a complete credit block; a florist opens a private gallery, taps agree, downloads. Cut to a vendor page: planners ranked by galleries taken and tag-backs. VO: 'Type the list once. Credits, galleries and terms handle themselves.' End card: 'Vendor Credits. $4 a month. Pre-launch, link in bio.'
