How it works

Each shoot

  • 60 to 90 minutes, 2 or 3 looks
  • One location, or two close together
  • Mornings 08:00–11:00 or late afternoon 16:30–19:00

Wardrobe

  • From the model's own wardrobe
  • A slip or summer dress, white shirt, denim, a swimsuit
  • Neutrals, whites, one strong colour

The model gets

  • Edited images from film and digital
  • Free to use for their book, the agency site and social
  • No fee in either direction
