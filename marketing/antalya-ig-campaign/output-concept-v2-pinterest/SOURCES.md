# Pinterest concept references

Research began with Mediterranean swimwear and coastal fashion campaign references, then moved to visual review directly in Pinterest. The final six images were gathered from the swimwear recommendation feed and the beach fashion editorial search, filtered to women.

Direction: natural beach light, sea blues, simple swimwear, light layers and relaxed poses. Images are visual references, not Aidan’s portfolio or photos of a confirmed Antalya location.
