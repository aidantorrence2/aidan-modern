# Concept slide references

Seven reference images, chosen on 14 Sept 2026 from a Pinterest search sweep anchored on Antalya, Kaş and the Turkish/Mediterranean coast (about 560 pins reviewed on contact sheets). Direction: sea air, simple styling, beach light, light layers. All are references for the mood, not Aidan's photos and not confirmed Antalya locations. Full-resolution originals are in `concept/refs/`.
