# Research — 2026 IG Reels trends informing Concept Set 85

Two cited research passes (full briefs in agent transcripts). Key, applied conclusions:

## Format
- **#1 photographer format for 2026 = BTS → final-photo reveal** ("you'd never guess", "the
  photo vs the BTS", expectation-vs-reality). Posting BTS is now a *credibility* move —
  audiences assume polished work is AI unless they see the process. Film grain reinforces "real."
- Retention/completion + replays are weighted far above length. 10s @ 80% beats 60s @ 30%.

## Length & pacing
- Sweet spot **7–15s** (highest retention); **15–30s** for narrative. Don't pad past 30s.
- Hook in the **first ~1.5s** (bold on-screen text — ~85% watch muted). Cut every **1.5–3s**.
- **Seamless loop** (last frame ≈ first frame) drives replays → built into 85e (and 85a echo).

## Audio (the big lever here)
- **Original-audio storytelling is algorithm-favored** and reads as authentic. The funny
  "how many people have died on this / hopefully it's zero" exchange is the unfair advantage →
  used as the spine of all 5 cuts, punchline timed to land on the reveal.
- Trending songs are NOT licensed for business accounts (muting/limited reach risk) → original
  audio (rights-clean) is the safe + best choice. RF fallback vibes if ever needed: dreamy
  indie/cinematic ~90–110 BPM (Pixabay CC0, Uppbeat, YT Audio Library).
- Master to **−14 LUFS**.

## Text / captions
- Heavy grotesque sans (Bebas/Anton/Montserrat). **Yellow + black outline** hooks (tested 3.4×
  more scroll-stopping). Burn in dialogue captions (muted autoplay).
- Safe zones: keep text out of **bottom ~320px** and **right ~90px**; hooks top-third,
  captions lower-center (~y1230–1350).

## CTA (model-search / free shoot)
- Soft, value-framed, **end-of-reel only**. Prefer **comment-a-keyword** over "DM me" (drives
  engagement + auto-DM). Gentle scarcity ("a few free sessions this month"). Rotate 3–5 DM
  reply variations; never blast identical strings (ban trigger).
- Keywords used: SHOOT (85a), FILM (85b), tag-a-friend (85c), GOLDEN (85d), BRIDGE (85e).
