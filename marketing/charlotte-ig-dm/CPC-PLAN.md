# Charlotte DM ads — CPC concept plan (21 Oct 2026, planning only, nothing rendered)

## 1. What CPC actually is here

cost per click = CPM ÷ (1000 × CTR). For a click-to-Instagram-Direct ad the click is the "Send message" tap.

Your own numbers (Istanbul / Antalya / Athens / Fethiye):
- CTR moved 10× between creatives (0.7% → 7.4%). Click→chat barely moved (15–18%). So the creative's job is the tap, and the tap is decided by card 1.
- CPM is the other half and is mostly *not* creative: country (US ≈ 2–4× Turkey), placement (Reels/Stories cheaper than Feed), audience (your women-only sets were your cheapest chats), and Meta's quality/engagement ranking (native-looking ads that get dwell, swipes, saves pay less).
- Realistic Charlotte floor: at a $8–12 CPM you need ≥4% CTR to get under $0.30 a click. Below 1.5% CTR nothing else matters.

So every concept below is a bet on one of three levers: **stop** (first frame), **intent** (the tap feels small and obvious), **CPM** (native, cheap placement, engagement).

## 2. The rule that trims the existing 40

Meta's ad standard on non-existent functionality bans images that imitate play buttons, notifications, checkboxes, multiple-choice options or any control that doesn't work. A disapproved ad has infinite CPC.

- **Run as-is (policy-safe):** H01/H02 controls, D03 band, E03 seal, E09 stacked grotesque, G04 text portrait, G05 stickers, G06 receipt, G07 proof sheet "you?", G08 lineup, G09 double exposure, G10 glitch, M07 weather, M10 newspaper, M11 ticket, M12 tarot, M13 trading card, M16 $0, M17 rings, M18 clapperboard, M21 album, M22 marquee, M23 neon, M24 chalkboard, M27 facts label, M28 postcard, M30 highway sign.
- **Strip the fake controls, then run:** G01 viewfinder (drop the shutter button; AF boxes are fine), G02 lock screen (keep clock + wallpaper, drop the notifications), G03 story (drop the drawn poll/question — use Meta's *real* poll sticker on a Stories ad instead), M04 FaceTime (drop Accept/Decline; "Charlotte is calling" alone still works), M05 now-playing (drop transport controls), M01/M02/M14/M15/M20 (drop Send/Accept/Follow/Message buttons), M29 video call (drop the bar + "tap to join").
- **Don't run:** M09 App Store (GET), M19 search page, M25 retro dialog buttons, M26 CAPTCHA (checkboxes + Verify), M06/M08 buttons. These are the policy text almost verbatim.

## 4. New concepts, by lever (none built yet)

### Stop — first frame
- **S1 Nothing.** 90% black card, one small print, "Charlotte" in 40px. A silent frame in a loud feed. Hypothesis: contrast beats brightness.
- **S2 Upside down.** The winner's hook rotated 180° with one line the right way up: "free photo shoot." People stop for broken things.
- **S3 The develop.** 4-second Reel: black → the portrait fades up over 1.5s, the city lands on the last frame. Motion in frame 1 for the Reels auction; no film talk.
- **S4 Neighbourhood hero.** Same winner hook, but the big word is **NoDa / South End / Plaza Midwood / Uptown** instead of Charlotte. Four ads. Micro-local relevance; also tells you which neighbourhood pulls.
- **S5 Eyes only, 1 second.** Reel: a 1s hold on eyes, then the full portrait, then the city. Shortest possible path to a face.

### Intent — make the tap small and obvious
- **I1 "just say hi."** Hook + one handwritten line: *just say hi 👋*. Tests whether naming the tiny action beats "Interested? Let's chat."
- **I2 The ice breakers, shown.** Card 2 lists the four real ice-breaker questions as handwriting (not chips): "is it really free?" / "when are you in Charlotte?" / "what should I wear?" / "how long does it take?". Tells them the DM is one tap, not a conversation they have to start.
- **I3 What you get.** One card: *~10 edited photos · about a week · $0* — needs your real numbers. Specificity is the strongest intent lever you haven't used.
- **I4 Dates.** Hook + *Oct 27 – Nov 9* (whatever is true). Honest deadline. Needs your dates.
- **I5 The reply.** Card 2 is a screenshot-style DM where Aidan's first reply is already written — the viewer sees the answer before they ask. (Bubbles are fine; no Send button.)

### CPM — native, cheap placement, engagement
- **C1 Aidan on camera, 10 seconds.** Still the single biggest lever you haven't pulled. Talking-to-camera gets the cheapest CPM and highest CTR of anything in this category. Shot list is in `marketing/charlotte-video-ads/HI-AIDAN-SHOTLIST.md`.
- **C2 Zero-design story.** The portrait, the city typed in Instagram's own text style, nothing else. Looks like a friend's story. Policy-safe version of G03.
- **C3 Real poll sticker.** A Stories ad with Meta's actual interactive poll ("want photos like these? yes / yes 👀"). Engagement counts toward ranking; it's a real control, so it's allowed.
- **C4 5-second loops.** Winner / Notes / Hi-Aidan cut to 5s, face big in frame 1, loop point clean. Watch-through rate feeds the ranking.
- **C5 Swipe-bait carousel.** Card 1 is a question ("what's the catch?"), card 2 the answer (the receipt). Swipes are engagement; engagement lowers CPM.

### Structure — not creative, but it moves CPC more than most creative
- **T1 Women-only ad set** vs all-genders, same 4 ads. Your data says women-only is cheapest.
- **T2 Placement split:** Reels-only vs Stories+Feed, same ads. Measures CPM per placement for Charlotte.
- **T3 Budget reality:** at $5/day you buy ~500–800 impressions a day. That's a CTR read on 2 ads, not 4. Either 2 ads per $5 set or $10–15/day for a 4-day read.
- **T4 Copy as its own test:** primary text (a) deck lines, (b) one line "Free photo shoot in Charlotte. Message me.", (c) question. Headline fixed: "Charlotte Free Photo Shoot". Changing text is free; it's a CTR lever people skip.

## 5. Suggested order (4 ads per round, 3–4 days each, no edits mid-round)

- **Round 1 (structure + baseline):** H01 control · G07 proof sheet · G06 receipt · G01 viewfinder (shutter removed). Women-only set. Primary text = deck lines.
- **Round 2 (intent):** keep the round-1 winner · I1 just say hi · I3 what you get (once numbers are confirmed) · S4 best neighbourhood.
- **Round 3 (CPM):** C1 Aidan on camera (if filmed) · C4 5s loop of the round-1 winner · C3 real poll sticker · S3 the develop.
- Kill rule: pause anything above 1.5× the control's CPC after 1,000 impressions. Judge on cost per click first, cost per chat once an ad has 5+ chats.
