What the research says

Format

9:16 Reels with text overlays, a person on screen and a hook in the first 2 seconds. In Meta's own study these had 20% lower CPC and 56% higher CTR. Reels and Stories also have the cheapest CPMs, which matters most at $5/day. Carousels go in the set as proof.

Trust is the bottleneck

"Free shoot" ads on Instagram are a known scam pattern: a free session, then a deposit or a package. Every ad names the catch ("I use the photos in my portfolio"), says no deposit or packages, and shows Aidan's real face and site.

Honest anchors

Charlotte portrait sessions run about $250–400 (Snappr, Shoott, Airbnb listings). Peak fall colour lasts until about mid-November, and sunset moves to about 5:20 PM after Nov 1. That gives a real deadline without inventing one.

Local signal

Neighbourhood names (NoDa, Uptown, South End, Camp North End, Freedom Park) say "local" more strongly than "Charlotte" alone. Locals say Uptown, not downtown. Ages 20–35; UNC Charlotte and CPCC alone have more than 66k students.

Campaign setup

Structure

  • 1 campaign: Engagement objective, conversion location Messaging apps → Instagram
  • 1 ad set: $5/day, goal "maximise conversations"
  • Charlotte +25 mi, ages 20–40, Advantage+ audience on
  • Placements: Instagram Reels, Stories, Feed
  • 10 ads (the launch set). Meta shifts spend to the winners

In the DM

  • Welcome: "Hey! I'm Aidan 👋 Free portrait sessions in Charlotte this month. No fees, no packages. What's your name, and which part of town are you in?"
  • Ice breakers: "I want a free shoot" · "How does it work?" · "Is it really free?" · "Can I bring a friend?"
  • Reply inside an hour while ads run

Testing 80 on $5/day

  • $5/day can't test 80 ads at once. Each ad would get pennies.
  • Week 1–2: launch set of 10. Pause anything under the median hook rate after about 1,000 impressions.
  • Every 10–14 days, swap the losers for wave 2, then wave 3
  • Expect roughly $2–8 per conversation (to verify on day 3)

Before anything goes live

Offer details to confirm

  1. Dates you are in Charlotte, and how many spots (the boards assume 10)
  2. What they get: number of edited photos and turnaround (boards say ~10, about a week)
  3. Whether free sessions are shot on film
  4. Duo/couple sessions: yes or no
  5. Whether the A-tier photos are from Paris (concept 18)
