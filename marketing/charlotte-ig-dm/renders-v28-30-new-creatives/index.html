CHARLOTTE / ORIGINAL PORTRAIT COLLABS

Ten new concepts.
Thirty invitations.

Three different copy/photo versions of each concept. Every card uses a different original portfolio photograph and a small bubble of Aidan's original profile photo. The decorative backgrounds are generated art; the photographs are direct source files and the lettering is editable.

Photo choices are an editorial testing shortlist after reviewing all 106 candidates. They are not measured engagement winners: no per-photo results were found.
