# Creative Brief: $5/day IG "Free Shoot" Ads, Charlotte NC (researched 2026-10-20)

Evidence note: almost no independent data exists on photographer model-call ads. The best data below is Meta's own Reels study (relayed by Adobe). Most other numbers come from vendor blogs and should be treated as rough direction only. Marked **[vendor]**.

---

## 1. Creative formats and patterns

**Format: lead with 9:16 Reels, and turn on Stories as a placement.**
- In Meta's analysis, Reels ads that were 9:16, had sound on, kept text inside the safe zone, and used at least one extra element (a person on screen, text/sticker overlays, voice-over, lo-fi footage, or a hook in the first few seconds) got **20% lower CPC, 56% higher CTR, 93% higher ThruPlay, and 30% higher 3-second play rate** ([Adobe/Meta](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/11/04/unlock-potential-reels-best-practices-for-creative-success-using-adobe-creative-cloud); [booyah, 10 Meta lift studies](https://www.booyah.com/blog/why-you-should-use-creative-designed-specifically-for-reels/)).
- One 2026 roundup gives CPMs of about Reels $6.20, Stories $6.60, carousel $8.50, feed image $9.00, and puts Reels CTR highest at about 1.35%. Other sources disagree a lot ([searchlab](https://searchlab.nl/en/statistics/instagram-statistics-2026), [1clickreport](https://www.1clickreport.com/blog/instagram-ads-dashboard-2026-guide)) **[vendor]**. At $5/day, low CPM matters most, which favors Reels and Stories.
- **Carousels** are a good secondary ad. A "swipe to see the edit" or 5-frame portfolio carousel works as proof for people who already saw the Reel. **Static** images are the weakest option for cold reach, but one strong film-look portrait with a text block is still a cheap test.
- Rough plan: 2–3 Reels variants plus 1 carousel. Creatives tire in about 3–6 weeks ([segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-creative-strategy-ecommerce-2026)) **[vendor]**. At $5/day, rotate a new hook every 2–3 weeks.

**Hooks (first 1–2 seconds).** Meta's guidance is to "nail the hook" in the first few seconds ([onlinemarketing.de summary of Meta's Reels mini-manual](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/meta-leidfaden-tipps-insights-herausragende-reels)). A common target hook rate (3-second views ÷ impressions) is above 30%, with under 25% counted as weak ([benly](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/meta-ads-reels-ads-guide)) **[vendor]**. Patterns to test, from strongest guess down:
1. **Local call-out on frame 1:** "I'm looking for 5 people in Charlotte to photograph this month." Naming the city and a number stops locals scrolling.
2. **BTS → reveal:** phone footage of the camera and location for about 1.5 seconds, then a hard cut to the graded film-look frame. This is Meta's "lo-fi plus person on screen" combination.
3. **Casting-call notice:** an on-screen card styled like a flyer ("CASTING: CLT / Free editorial portrait session / Nov dates").
4. **POV:** "POV: a stranger DMs you for a free film shoot in NoDa," followed by results.
5. **Before/after:** an iPhone snap of the person next to the final edit, which answers "will I look good?"
- Talking to the camera is widely called the top hook format **[vendor, unsourced]** ([admakeai](https://admakeai.com/blog/ugly-ads-outperform-polished)). Aidan appearing on screen also builds trust that he is a real person (see §2).

**Lo-fi/UGC vs polished.** Vendor reports say UGC-style ads cut CPA about 23% and lift CTR up to 48% ([growwithsakib](https://growwithsakib.com/ugc-ads-facebook/)) **[vendor]**. Others say polish still signals quality at premium price points ([loop.fans](https://loop.fans/blog/ugc-ads-vs-studio-ads-why-authentic-content-wins)). **Best mix for a photographer:** a lo-fi wrapper (phone BTS, Aidan talking) around polished payoff frames, since the photos themselves are the product.

**Text overlays.** Meta counts text/sticker overlays as a lift element ([Adobe/Meta](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/11/04/unlock-potential-reels-best-practices-for-creative-success-using-adobe-creative-cloud)). Keep each card to 1 idea and about 6 words, use high contrast (white Georgia on a dark scrim fits the brand), and make it readable with sound off. Primary text gets cut off at about 72 characters on Reels ([Scribd copy of Meta Reels guide](https://www.scribd.com/document/860254779/Meta-Reels-Ads-Guide)), so put the offer first.

**Safe zones (1080×1920).** Margins credited to Meta: **top 14% (~270 px), bottom 35% (~670 px) for Reels ads, sides 6% (~65 px).** Stories ads need about 20% (~380 px) clear at the bottom ([hopperhq](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/), [houseofmarketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/guide-to-safe-zones-tiktok-facebook-instagram-stories-reels/), [inro](https://www.inro.social/tools/instagram-reels-safe-zone-checker)). Organic Reels are more forgiving (about 220–250 px top and bottom), but **design to the ad spec: keep text and faces within y≈270–1250 px.** Check with the safe-zone overlay in Ads Manager.

**CTAs.**
- Use a **Click-to-Instagram-Direct objective** with a pre-filled icebreaker ("I want a free shoot"). Meta reports click-to-message ads lowered cost per lead 27% in one case study ([tradeinformer/Meta Equiti](https://www.tradeinformer.com/broker-news/equiti-sees-36-increase-in-leads-27-cost-reduction-using-meta-messenger-ads)).
- "**DM me 'SHOOT'**" or "Comment SHOOT": Meta is testing a native "Reply to Keywords" auto-DM on ad comments, with up to 5 keywords ([Social Media Today](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-tests-auto-dm-response-for-instagram-ad-comments/828791)). Requiring a keyword filters for intent ([wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/instagram-comment-automation/)). Public comments also add social proof under the ad.
- **Price anchor:** "Sessions like this run $250–$400 in Charlotte. This month: free." Use the real market range from §4, not an inflated number.
- **Scarcity:** give a real limit, e.g. "5 spots · Nov 1–16 · before the leaves drop." Peak foliage timing gives a true deadline.
- **Objections, one card each:** "No modeling experience needed — I direct every pose." "You keep the edited photos." "No fees, no packages, ever."

## 2. Why free-shoot ads fail, and how to frame "why free"

**Why they fail.** The "free shoot" scam pattern is well known. Someone gets a free session, then is pushed into a deposit or a package of about £1,000, or gets no photos unless they buy ([reddit r/MODELING](https://reddit.birdcat.cafe/r/MODELING/comments/1i1vrgl/are_uk_models_legit), [Trustpilot reviews](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/voxtalent.co.uk?page=2)). Commenters specifically call **Instagram ads** for shoots a red flag. Safety worries are real too: impersonated photographers and models going missing after jobs ([Fstoppers](https://fstoppers.com/commercial/dangerous-situation-models-and-photographers-3183)). The scam signals are deposits, paid portfolios, upsells, a business you can't verify, and unclear image rights.

**Countermeasures to show in the ad or the first DM reply:**
- State the catch plainly: *"The catch: I get to use the photos in my portfolio. That's it."* TFP/test shoots are a normal industry trade ([Format](https://www.format.com/magazine/tfp-shoot)).
- **No money at any point.** Say "no deposit, no packages" outright. That is the line between a test shoot and a scam.
- Face on camera, real name, and a link to aidantorrence.com and the IG grid. A verifiable person counters the "unregistered company" pattern.
- **Safety:** "Bring a friend — totally welcome." Public locations only, plus a written usage/release agreement ([Format](https://www.format.com/magazine/tfp-shoot), [Sirency](https://www.sirency.com/blog/tfp-test-shoot-guide)).
- Give a delivery promise: edited images within about 1 week, which Format calls the TFP norm.

**Credible "why free" framings** (pick one true one and repeat it). Model calls are a standard way to find fresh faces and prove a style to future clients ([Unscripted](https://unscriptedphotographers.com/blog/how-to-create-a-drool-worthy-portfolio)).
- "I'm building a Charlotte portfolio for a new film-look series."
- "I'm testing a new look / film stock / color grade and need real faces, not models."
- "New to CLT — I want to shoot the city's people before I open bookings."
A specific, slightly selfish reason is more believable than vague generosity.

## 3. Charlotte specifics

**Locations** ([Shoott 2026](https://www.shoott.com/blog/best-photoshoot-locations-in-charlotte-a-local-photographers-guide-2026), [Shoott](https://www.shoott.com/blog/the-best-photoshoot-locations-in-charlotte), [Charlotte's Got A Lot](https://www.charlottesgotalot.com/articles/things-to-do/15-places-for-the-perfect-charlotte-wedding-picture), [NAPCP](https://www.napcp.com/2021/01/11/urban-industrial-session-share-by-carolyn-ann-ryan/), [Travel Noire](https://travelnoire.com/5-instagram-worthy-spots-charlotte-nc)):
- **Uptown / Romare Bearden Park** (300 S Church St): the classic skyline-plus-green shot. Weekday mornings are quieter. A pro permit "may be required" ([Snappr](https://www.snappr.com/photo-locations/graduation/charlotte-nc)).
- **NoDa:** murals and brick, free street parking. The top urban-editorial pick.
- **Camp North End:** "the go-to place for creatives," with rail tracks, a skyline view, murals on shipping containers and dock doors. Busy on weekends, and parts are under construction.
- **South End / Rail Trail:** light-rail urban energy and the Evelyn Henson "Confetti Hearts" wall.
- **Freedom Park** (East Blvd): lake, lawns, fall trees. Lots fill on weekends.
- **Midtown Park / Little Sugar Creek Greenway:** clean modern lines and golden light.
- **Duke Mansion** (Myers Park): 1915 estate with 4.5 acres of gardens. A private venue, so ask first.
- Also: Plaza Midwood (eclectic, but no strong mural sources found), the Bechtler Museum facade, Ballantyne, and Lake Norman/Jetton Park.
- Permits: I found no Mecklenburg County photo-permit rule online. Confirm with Park & Rec before naming a park in ads. NC *state* parks do require permits ([NC Admin Code](https://regulations.justia.com/states/north-carolina/title-07/chapter-13/subchapter-b/section-1100/section-13b-1102)).

**Local language:** "Queen City," "the QC," "CLT," "Hornet's Nest," "Crown Town," "Buzz City," "the 704." Locals say **"Uptown," not "downtown"** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte,_North_Carolina), [Sparefoot](https://www.sparefoot.com/moving/city-guides/4385376-how-to-sound-like-a-local-in-charlotte/), [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/78371/25-things-you-should-know-about-charlotte)). Charlotte is in **North** Carolina; locals care about that ([CLTtoday](https://clttoday.6amcity.com/city-guide/live/unwritten-rules-charlotte-nc)). Neighborhood names (NoDa, South End, Plaza Midwood, Uptown, Camp North End) are stronger geo-signals in copy than "Charlotte" alone.

**Demographics:** city population about 886k, median age **34.4** (ACS 2023) ([wtfvote/ACS](https://wtfvote.us/census/north-carolina/cities/charlotte)). Regional median age is 35.5 and the metro added about 289k residents 2020–25 ([UNC Charlotte UI](https://ui.charlotte.edu/2026/05/13/what-the-2025-census-tells-us-about-charlottes-changing-population/), [jaredwatkins](https://www.jaredwatkins.com/research/relocation/charlotte-nc/)). South End is about 32.8 within 1 mile ([CRE profile](https://www.collettre.com/images/Properties/8841_Circumferential_Rd_Demos__Nov_2025_.pdf)). Many residents are young transplants. Colleges:
- **UNC Charlotte:** 32,207 students, a fall 2025 record ([UNC Charlotte](https://ucomm.charlotte.edu/?p=36383)).
- **CPCC:** more than 34,000, a fall 2025 record ([CPCC](https://www.cpcc.edu/news/central-piedmont-hits-record-enrollment-34000-students-expands-programs-meet-charlottes-workforce-demands)).
- **Queens University:** about 1,600, merging with Elon around summer 2026 ([Bond Buyer](https://www.bondbuyer.com/articles/private-north-carolina-universities-announce-a-merger)).
- **JCSU:** about 1,000 undergrads ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/newsletters/axios-charlotte-304cd2f2-85c3-4fcd-b731-76ff8b33e330.html?stream=top)).
Target **ages 20–35**. Fall-grad and senior photos give a seasonal reason to want a shoot.

**Late Oct / Nov conditions:**
- **Foliage:** peak in Charlotte is roughly late October to the first week of November, sometimes into mid-November ([WCNC](https://wcnc.com/article/weather/autumn-colors-peak-fall-foliage-in-nc/275-4d5ce878-e1a1-43d2-a2b1-63f21ffdee84), [CLTtoday](https://clttoday.6amcity.com/travel-and-outdoors/peak-fall-foliage-charlotte-nc)). That makes "shoot before the leaves drop" an honest scarcity hook for the **next 2–3 weeks**.
- **Weather:** November highs fall from about 67°F to 58°F and lows from about 47°F to 40°F. Rain totals about 3 inches, with a wet-day chance near 20–23% ([WeatherSpark](https://weatherspark.com/m/18893/11/Average-Weather-in-November-in-Charlotte-North-Carolina-United-States)). It's jacket and layers weather, which suits editorial styling.
- **Light:** DST ends **Sun Nov 1, 2026**, which moves sunset from about 6:30 PM to about 5:20 PM. November sunsets average about 5:30 PM with about 10.4 hours of daylight ([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/weather/58193/11/charlotte-weather-in-november)). Golden-hour slots will fall around 4–5 PM, which works for students and remote workers but is hard for 9-to-5 workers on weekdays. Offer weekend slots.

## 4. Charlotte portrait pricing (for an honest anchor)
- Snappr estimates for Charlotte portraits: about **$197 for 1 hour, $315 for 2 hours, $551 for 4 hours** ([Snappr](https://snappr.com/how-much-does-a-photographer-cost/portrait/charlotte-nc), updated Dec 2024).
- Headshot studio: $325 session + $100 per image ([Aleigha Tucker](https://aleighatuckerphotography.mypixieset.com/headshot-pricing-charlotte-north-carolina/)). LR Morgan: from $300, plus $100 per image and $200+ on-location ([Cliqora](https://www.cliqora.com/photographer/lr-morgan-photography-charlotte)).
- Airbnb Charlotte listings: $250 branding (1 hr); $400 luxury portrait (90 min, 20+ edits); $600 branding (2 hr, 20 images) ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/services/6081171)). Shoott: $299 for a 30-min full gallery ([Shoott](https://www.shoott.com/blog/best-photoshoot-locations-in-charlotte-a-local-photographers-guide-2026)). Thumbtack: elaborate shoots run $600–$700 ([Thumbtack](https://www.thumbtack.com/nc/charlotte/portrait-photographers/)).
- **Honest anchor:** "A session like this is typically $250–$400 in Charlotte." Avoid single numbers above about $400 unless the deliverables really match them (multiple looks, 20+ retouched images).
