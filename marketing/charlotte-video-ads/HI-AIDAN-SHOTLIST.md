# "Hi, I'm Aidan" — real selfie version (10–12 s)

`variants/b-hi-aidan.mjs` is a photo-motion stand-in. A real talking-to-camera clip usually beats it, so if you can film
one, this is the whole shoot. Phone, front camera, vertical 9:16, 4K/30, natural light facing a window.

| time | shot | what you say (or on-screen text if you'd rather not talk) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | face to camera, chest up, already mid-wave | "hi, I'm Aidan." |
| 2–5 s | same take, small step closer | "I'm a portrait photographer, and I'm in Charlotte for a few weeks." |
| 5–8 s | cut: the portraits (render keeps the montage from the stand-in) | (music only) |
| 8–10 s | back to you, smiling, camera in hand | "I'd love to photograph you — it's free." |
| 10–12 s | point down at the button | "tap and say hi." |

Notes:
- Keep your face in the upper half of the frame: captions go in the lower half and must never cover a face.
- One continuous take for 0–5 s works better than cuts. Re-record until it sounds like a voice note, not a script.
- Don't mention film, dates or "models".
- Drop the clip in `work/src/aidan-hi.mov`; the variant then swaps the selfie still for the clip (small change).
