## Licences in one paragraph

* **Music beds** are original arrangements made from Apple Loops that ship with GarageBand/Logic (`/Library/Audio/Apple Loops/Apple/`). Apple's software licence lets you use the included Apple Loops royalty-free in your own original musical compositions and soundtracks, including commercial video; the loops may not be redistributed on their own (as loops/samples). Each bed layers 5-7 loops with its own arrangement (entries, drop-outs, filter sweeps, sidechain-style pumping, reverb, EQ), so no loop is used raw.
* **SFX** come from Wikimedia Commons files licensed **CC0** or **public domain** only (no attribution needed), or are synthesised by the build script (no third-party material).
