## Steps
1. **Charge it outside the bedroom.** Pick one spot — kitchen counter, hallway. Not the nightstand.
2. **Set a hard cutoff.** Same time every night (example: 10:30). Use your phone's built-in Downtime / Bedtime mode.
3. **Turn on grayscale after cutoff.** Colour is part of what makes feeds pull you back in; grey makes them boring.
4. **Make it physical if willpower fails.** A timed lock box means you can't "just check one thing".
