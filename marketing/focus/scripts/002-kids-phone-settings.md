## Steps

2. **App limits by category.** Social and games get a daily cap; school apps don't.
3. **Content & privacy restrictions.** Block explicit content and require approval for new app downloads.
4. **Keep the passcode yours.** The settings only work if your kid can't change them.
5. **(Bonus) Talk first.** Tell them what you're setting up and why — it lands better than a surprise.
