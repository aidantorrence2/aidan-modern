## Steps
1. **Set it up as a child account,** not with your own login — the controls depend on it.
2. **Decide the phone's bedtime now.** Where it charges at night and from what time. Easier to start with a rule than add one later.
3. **Start with fewer apps.** Add them one at a time, with approval, as they show they can handle it.
4. **Write a one-page phone agreement together.** What's okay, what isn't, and what happens if it's broken.
