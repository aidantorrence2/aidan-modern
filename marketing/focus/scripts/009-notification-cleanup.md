## Hook (0–3 s)
"Your phone buzzes. Was it a person, or an app that wants you back?"

## Steps
1. **Open your notification settings** and scroll the whole list. Most people have never done it.
2. **Keep only people:** calls, messages, family. Everything else goes off.
3. **Kill badges too.** Red dots are notifications that don't make a sound.
4. **Check apps on purpose,** at times you pick — not when they ping you.
