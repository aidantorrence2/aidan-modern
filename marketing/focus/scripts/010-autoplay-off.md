## Steps
1. **Turn off autoplay** in every video app your kid uses — it's usually under Settings → Playback.
2. **Use the kids' version** of the app where one exists; fewer rabbit holes.
3. **Set a daily time limit** for video apps specifically, not the whole device.
4. **End on a clear signal.** "Two episodes, then the tablet goes on the shelf" beats "a little longer."
