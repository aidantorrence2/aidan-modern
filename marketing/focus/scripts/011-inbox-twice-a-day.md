# 011 — Check email twice a day (and still not miss anything)

## Hook (0–3 s)
"Checking email every few minutes isn't being responsive. It's being interrupted."

## Steps
1. **Pick two check-in times** — e.g. 11:00 and 16:00. Put them in your calendar.
2. **Close the email tab** and turn off email notifications between them.
3. **Block email on a schedule** outside those times, on laptop and phone.
4. **Tell people how to reach you if it's urgent** — a call or a message. Urgent things rarely live in email anyway.
