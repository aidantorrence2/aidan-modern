## Hook (0–3 s)

## Steps
1. **Set a daily play limit on the console itself** — most consoles have family settings for this.
2. **Match it on the phone and tablet,** or the game just moves screens.
3. **Give a 10-minute warning** before the limit, so they can finish the round.
4. **Agree on the rule once, out loud.** Then the timer enforces it, not you.
