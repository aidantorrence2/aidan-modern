## Hook (0–3 s)
"Everyone at the table, nobody actually there? Try this for one week."

## Steps
1. **One basket, by the door.** Every phone goes in before anyone sits down — adults first.
2. **Silence, don't switch off.** Calls from family still come through.
3. **Give the table something to do:** one question per meal, from a list on the fridge.
4. **Same rule for guests,** said kindly. Most people are relieved.
