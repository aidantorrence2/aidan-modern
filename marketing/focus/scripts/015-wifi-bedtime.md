## Steps
1. **Open your router's app or settings page** and look for "schedule" or "parental controls."
2. **Pause only the kids' devices** at bedtime, not the whole house.
3. **Remember mobile data:** a router schedule doesn't cover it — the phone's own settings do.
4. **Announce it once,** and let the schedule do the reminding.
