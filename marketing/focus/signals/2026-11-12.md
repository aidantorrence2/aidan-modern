# Signals — 2026-11-12 (first pass, web search only)

Scope: 3 web searches (doomscrolling fixes, parents/teen screen-time rules, Aura affiliate terms). No platform analytics, no ad-library access, no Reddit API — treat as directional. Sources listed at the bottom.

## What people report actually working (adults)
1. **"I blocked the app and just used the browser."** Recurring failure mode in r/Stopscrolling and blocker write-ups. → Our scripts already cover it (003 step 3, 004 step 3, 014 step 2). **Make it the hook** in a new script: "You blocked the app. Then opened the website. Here's the fix."
2. **Change the feed, not just the time.** One user's fix: reset Instagram's suggested content and turn off YouTube watch/browse history. → New script angle (no offer needed; trust builder): "Reset your feed in 2 minutes." Keep menu paths generic.
3. **Streaks / social commitment.** Streak widgets and group-commitment apps mentioned as motivating. → Possible CTA angle: "7-day challenge — comment the keyword to join." (Needs owner to want a community element.)
4. **Phone fully off / cold turkey** mentioned as hard but effective. Consistent with 001/013 framing.
5. Vendor blogs dominate search results; their "best app" rankings are marketing — don't repeat their claims.

## What parents are told works (articles/studies, not Reddit — search returned no Reddit threads)
1. **Precise, co-created rules** (time, duration, place) beat vague ones; teen involvement helps. → Matches 008 (agreement), 012 (agree once, out loud). Lean into "make the rule together" in hooks.
2. **Routines over caps:** phones near the bed at sleep and at meals associated with more use (ABCD study coverage). → Supports 001, 013, 015. **Do not put the study's numbers on screen** unless the owner wants to cite and link the primary source.
3. **Parent modeling** matters. → New parent script angle: "Your kid copies your screen time — 3 things to change first (yours)." Low-sell, high-share.
4. **Screen time as reward/punishment didn't help** in that study coverage. → New script angle: "Stop using screen time as a reward. Do this instead." Must phrase as "researchers found it didn't help", link source in caption if used.
5. **Parental controls framed as a safety net + conversation tool, not surveillance.** → Good framing for KIDS/FIRST/AUTOPLAY/GAMES/WIFI offer cards; avoid "monitor everything" language. Already consistent.
