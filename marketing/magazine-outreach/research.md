## How pre-shoot pitches work

- Lead with a one-PDF proposal. Teeth asks for team list with portfolio links, photo and styling references, brand list, hair/makeup imagery, location, lighting, casting options, shoot and turnaround dates.
- Concept plus named team is the minimum. Schön! wants visuals and descriptions with suggested team and talent, each with clickable portfolio links, and aims to answer in 72 hours.
- Approval is 'for consideration', not a guarantee (Schön!, Teeth).
- Pull letters come from smaller titles, with conditions: IRK on emailed request with website, stylist name and moodboard; Flanelle charges; Basic refuses.
- Editors are wary because teams vanish (Flanelle: ~95% of pull-letter shoots never came back). Give exact dates and a final team, and deliver to the title that backed you.
- Exclusivity often covers social media (C-Heads, 33, Schön!). Hold all posts until release.
- Multi-brand credits are a hard filter (Purplehaze, 33, PAP). Kaltblut needs 4 looks / 10 pictures; PhotoBook 6 looks.
- Send small files; silence is a no. The team carries all shoot costs and insurance.
