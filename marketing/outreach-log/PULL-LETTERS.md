## Proposal PDF checklist

One clean PDF that works for every title above:

1. Story title and a concept of up to 250 words.
2. Mood boards for photography, styling, hair and makeup, and lighting.
3. Team with names, roles, Instagram and portfolio links: photographer, stylist, HMU, model with agency profile, assistants.
4. Brands the stylist plans to pull from, with several designers, and 8–12 looks.
5. Location, with reference photos.
6. Exact shoot date and call time, plus delivery date. Most titles need these to be final before they issue a letter.
7. Target placement (print or online, issue or theme) and format (35mm film and/or digital; Teeth is film only).
8. A line confirming the story is exclusive and unpublished.

Send a pull-letter pitch only once the stylist and dates are confirmed. Several titles issue one letter per exact team and date.
