A WOMAN IN TRANSIT. A WARDROBE WITH ITS OWN HISTORY.
One woman moves through an ordinary New York day without a fixed destination.
On the street, at a doorway and in the pause between places, her clothes shift
from protection to expression: a long coat becomes a moving shape, a structured
jacket softens against a wall, and an evening silhouette appears in daylight.
The city is a lived setting rather than a landmark backdrop. The photographs move
between full silhouettes and intimate portraits, pairing the texture of 35mm film
with a digital edit. The mood is self-possessed, tactile and slightly restless; the
clothing remains legible in every sequence.
PROPOSED SCOPE
One model. Eight looks. A compact exterior route. A proposed edit of 10-12
photographs, shaped with the selected stylist and publication.
STATUS
Creative direction proposed for review. Aidan's New York travel window is
confirmed. Shoot day, team, casting, locations, brands and publication remain
unconfirmed.

PHOTOGRAPHY
Proposed 35mm film and digital. Mostly natural light: open shade, soft
backlight and a brief harder-light sequence if conditions permit. Mix
full-length, three-quarter, portrait and detail frames. Keep grading
coherent and skin natural.
BEAUTY
Proposed understated skin, defined brows and lived-in hair that can
move. One subtle change for the closing looks. Hair and makeup artist
still to be cast; these are creative notes, not an agreed service.
LOCATION BRIEF
New York exterior textures: stone or painted walls, a threshold, glass
reflections and a quiet walking stretch. Prefer one compact route after a
scout. No specific property access, permission, changing space or
location booking is assumed.
PROPOSED MODEL / TEAM
One agency model, the stylist, hair and makeup, and Aidan as
photographer. Agency casting is prospective. No model, stylist or
additional crew is booked. Stylist interest is not a confirmed team.
