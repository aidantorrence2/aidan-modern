A WOMAN IN TRANSIT.
A WARDROBE WITH ITS OWN HISTORY.
One woman moves through a New York day without a fixed
destination. At a doorway, beside a window, in a pause on the
street: her clothes move between protection and expression.
Built from the photographic language of Aidan's portfolio: direct
presence, tactile black clothing, expressive hands, close portraits
and the color of real surroundings. The city provides texture and
tension; the person holds the frame.
PROPOSED SCOPE
One model. Eight looks developed with a stylist. A compact exterior route. A
proposed edit of 10-12 photographs on 35mm film and digital.
Creative proposal. Shoot day, casting, team, wardrobe, location access and publication
remain to be agreed.

Long coat, fine knit, fluid trouser.

05
A compact street production
PHOTOGRAPHY
35mm film and digital. Alternate a clear full look with a medium frame
and a close portrait. Start in open shade; add short directional-light
sequences when flattering. Preserve skin and fabric detail while allowing
the existing color of the scene to come through.
BEAUTY + PERFORMANCE
Natural skin, defined brows and hair that can move. Quiet confidence
rather than a fixed smile. Hands at a collar, a lean, a turn or a step give
the body something simple to do. Beauty and posing notes to refine with
the model and HMU artist.
LOCATION BRIEF
One walkable New York route with stone or painted walls, a threshold,
reflective glass and a quieter stretch. Scout first and verify access,
weather cover, bathrooms and a private changing option. Website
photographs describe visual qualities; they do not establish New York
locations.
PROPOSED TEAM
Aidan as photographer, one model, a stylist, and hair and makeup.
Casting and collaborators remain unconfirmed. Keep the working route
small enough to move between setups without repeated transport.
WORKING SHOOT FLOW
Allow roughly 5-6 hours including beauty, fitting, eight looks, changes and
breaks. Capture the essential full look and portrait first, then experiment.
Set the actual call time and light order after the day, route and team are
agreed.
DATE + EDIT
Proposed shoot within 21-28 October 2026, subject to the final New York
plan and team agreement. Build a 10-12 image sequence from
establishing silhouette to intimate portrait. Delivery date, magazine fit,
exclusivity and usage terms require agreement before any commitment.
The colored-light reference on page 3 is optional. It adds an interior or lighting
requirement only if selected for this production.
