Editorial concept · Athens · 1–2 October 2026

Athens,
as it is.

The laiki, Varvakios, the periptero, the kafeneio, Piraeus, Omonia.

An editorial set inside the working city. The model is dressed at full fashion pitch and placed where nobody dresses up: fish counters, kiosks, kafeneia, the harbour at dawn, Omonia after dark. The people who work there stay in the frame. Shot on 35mm film in natural light.

Aidan Torrence · aidantorrence.com · @madebyaidan
Neighbourhood street markets

The laiki

Crates, awnings, weighing scales, vendors calling prices. Couture against produce: tulle, loud prints, red boots, a headscarf.

Varvakios, the central market

Meat & fish

Athens' central meat and fish hall. Fluorescent light, ice, hanging carcasses, butchers in white. Rubber, chainmail, pearls and knitwear against steel counters.

The kiosk on every corner

The periptero

Walls of newspapers, cigarettes and gum, the owner leaning out of the hatch. Tailoring, fur and print crouched among the racks.

Greek coffee, tavli, the regulars

The kafeneio

Plastic chairs and the men who have sat there since morning. Lace, fur and silk at a table of regulars, and a yiayia next to the model.

The harbour

Piraeus

Fishing boats, nets and folding chairs on the harbour wall. Wide hats, waders, heavy knits and trench coats among the fishermen.

After dark

Omonia

Metro platforms, stairwells, arcades and neon. Fur, leather and a red coat under street light.

Styling
Full fashion in working places. Statement outerwear: fur, rubber, leather, sharp tailoring. Texture on texture: tulle, lace, knit, chainmail, silk. Accessories as character: headscarves, pearls, rubber gloves, fishnets, kitten heels on wet tiles. Greek labels, archive pieces and flea-market finds.
Hair & make-up
Undone hair, bare skin, one strong lip.
Camera
35mm film and digital, natural and available street light. Concept built together with the stylist.
