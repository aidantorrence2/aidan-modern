Editorial concept · Athens · 1–2 October 2026

Athens,
as it is.

The laiki, Varvakios, the periptero, the kafeneio, Piraeus, Omonia.

A fashion story at the level of i-D, Hunger and CR Fashion Book, set inside the working city. Designer and archive pieces go where nobody dresses up: fish counters, delivery trucks, kiosks, kafeneia, the harbour, Omonia after dark. The people who work there stay in the frame. Shot on 35mm film.

Aidan Torrence · aidantorrence.com · @madebyaidan
Neighbourhood street markets

The laiki

Couture in the aisles between crates. A tulle gown bent over a stall, a fur-hemmed coat with a headscarf, a body buried under a pile of clothes.

Varvakios, the central market

Meat & fish

Fluorescent light, ice and steel. Plastic and sequins against the catch, a fish carried as a handbag, a face pressed into a butcher’s tray.

The kiosk on every corner

The periptero

Newspapers, magazines, cigarettes, fridges of soda. Tailoring and bright colour pressed against the racks.

Kafeneio, yiayia, the regulars

The regulars

The model beside the people who live there: an old man and his dog, a seamstress behind her stall, a chair in a back room, an icon corner.

The harbour

Piraeus

Nets, floats, rope and grey water. Neon tailoring, armour-like knits and sculptural pleats against working gear.

After dark

Omonia

Direct flash, wet asphalt, sodium light. A trapper hat and puffer, a hooded red suit crossing the road, a red gown against a steel shutter.

Stoa, pavement, scaffolding

The street

The body as the idea. Hanging from scaffolding in a fringed dress, balancing on a bollard, folded in half beside a parked car, leaning on a roadworks barrier.

When
1–2 October 2026, Athens
Casting
Women from Ace Models
Styling
Designer and archive pieces at full volume: Miu Miu, Margiela, Comme des Garçons, Loewe, Simone Rocha, Mary Katrantzou. Proportion pushed: tulle, fringe, sculptural pleats, oversized outerwear. Working uniform as accessory: butcher’s whites, rubber gloves, waders, a headscarf. Market-stall finds mixed in.
Hair & make-up
Undone hair, bare skin, one strong detail.
Camera
35mm film and digital. Daylight, and direct flash after dark. Concept built together with the stylist.
