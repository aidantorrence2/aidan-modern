Editorial concept · Athens

Athens,
as it is.

The laiki, the periptero, the kafeneio, the port.

A women's fashion story set inside everyday Athens. Serious clothes in real places: lace, sequins, tailoring and brocade worn at the fruit stall, the kiosk, the bakery counter and the harbour wall, with the people who work there left in frame. Shot on 35mm film in natural light.

Aidan Torrence · aidantorrence.com · @madebyaidan
Laiki & Monastiraki

The market

Neighbourhood street markets and the Monastiraki flea market. Crates, awnings, fruit, stall owners at work.

StylingEvening pieces in daylight. A sequin slip on a fruit crate, a pleated ruffle dress at the banana cart, red patent boots by the produce van. Loafers and kitten heels, never trainers.

Periptero & kafeneio

The regulars

Kiosks, bakeries, souvlaki counters, old kafeneia with tavli boards. The owners and the old men stay in the picture.

StylingVolume and print against plastic chairs and steel counters. A floral ball dress at the shop counter, clashing prints in the bakery, a men’s tuxedo among the regulars, velvet and jewels at a café table.

Piraeus

The port

Ferries, fishing boats, men with lines off the harbour wall, rope and rust.

StylingCouture against work. A brocade coat among fishing rods, a lace slip on a café chair at the sea wall, a white trench on the quay, a sculptural corset and wide hat on the pilings.

Exarchia, Psyrri, Kypseli

The streets

Back streets, dented cars, scooters, peeling walls and the neighbours on their doorsteps.

StylingBlack lace and leather, a red column dress against a dented car. Hair and make-up kept real; the clothes do the talking.

The plan

The shoot

Casting
Women, cast through Ace Models
Styling
Designer and vintage at full strength: lace, sequins, brocade, tailoring, leather. Greek vintage and flea-market finds as accents, never as the whole look.
Locations
A laiki, a periptero, a kafeneio, Piraeus, the back streets. Locals asked to stay in frame.
Camera
35mm film and digital, natural light
Dates
To set together with the stylist
