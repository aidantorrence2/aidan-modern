Editorial concept · Athens · 1–2 October 2026

Athens,
as it is.

The laiki, the periptero, the kafeneio, the port.

An editorial set inside everyday Athens rather than in front of it. The model lives in real places, with the people who work there in frame: the fruit seller, the kiosk owner, the old men over tavli, the fishermen at Piraeus. Shot on 35mm film in natural light. Styling that feels found, not placed.

Aidan Torrence · aidantorrence.com · @madebyaidan

The market
