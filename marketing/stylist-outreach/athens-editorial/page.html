Editorial concept · Athens · 1–2 October 2026

Athens,
as it is.

The laiki, the periptero, the kafeneio, the port.

An editorial set inside everyday Athens rather than in front of it. The model lives in real places, with the people who work there in frame: the fruit seller, the kiosk owner, the old men over tavli, the fishermen at Piraeus. Shot on 35mm film in natural light. Styling that feels found, not placed.

Aidan Torrence · aidantorrence.com · @madebyaidan
Laiki & Monastiraki

The market

Neighbourhood street markets and the Monastiraki flea market. Crates, awnings, stall owners, antiques piled on pavements. The model shops, eats, waits, bargains.

Periptero & kafeneio

The regulars

Kiosks plastered with newspapers and cigarettes, souvlaki counters, old kafeneia with plastic chairs and tavli boards. Locals stay in the frame.

Piraeus

The port

Ferries, rope, fishing boats, men with lines off the harbour wall. Salt, rust, work clothes against fashion.

Exarchia & Psyrri

The walls

Tagged shutters, faded posters, motorbikes parked on the pavement, hand-painted shop signs. The city talking back.
