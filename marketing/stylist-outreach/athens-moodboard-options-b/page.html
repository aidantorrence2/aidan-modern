Athens moodboard options

Five directions for the Athens editorial, 1–2 October. One mood each, eight references.

OPTION 1

Black dress, white stone

Black and white, hard southern sun, a black dress against old walls. After Ferdinando Scianna.

OPTION 2

Hard light

Saturated colour, deep shadow, a single strong shape. After Viviane Sassen.

OPTION 3

Flash

Direct flash, deadpan, ordinary rooms and tiles. After Juergen Teller.

OPTION 4

Wind & volume

Sculptural coats and gowns, sea wind, open sky. After Jamie Hawkesworth and Harley Weir.

OPTION 5

Nocturne

Green-blue dusk, hotel rooms, fog, city glow. After Glen Luchford.

Reference images are for mood and styling only.
