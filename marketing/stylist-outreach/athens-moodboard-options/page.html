Athens moodboard options

Five directions for the Athens editorial, 1–2 October. Each is one theme with eight references.

OPTION 1

Greek streets

Graffiti, polykatoikia balconies, shutters, scaffolding, church steps. Designer pieces worn like everyday clothes on the pavement.

OPTION 2

The laiki

Street-market couture. Tulle, fur hems and headscarves between the crates, vendors in the frame.

OPTION 3

Varvakios

The central meat and fish market. Plastic, sequins and rubber against ice, steel and the catch.

OPTION 4

Piraeus

The harbour. Neon tailoring, knits and sculptural pieces among nets, floats and fishermen.

OPTION 5

After dark

Omonia at night. Direct flash, wet asphalt, fur, leather and red under street light.

Reference images are for mood and styling only.
