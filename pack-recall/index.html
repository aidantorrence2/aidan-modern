Is this just another checklist app?

Each trip's list is built from your notes on the last trip: what you used, what you skipped and what you missed. It is not a generic master list of 800 items.

Does it work for trips with kids?

Yes. You can keep a separate list for each person and tick them off one at a time.

Can it remind me to pack my medication?

You can add any item, including prescriptions, to your list. It only helps you remember to pack things. For questions about your medication, ask your doctor or pharmacist.
