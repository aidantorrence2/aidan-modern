How to avoid being charged for hire-car damage you didn't cause

Disputes over hire-car damage usually come down to evidence. A few minutes with your phone at both ends of the hire makes a big difference. This is general information, not legal advice.

At pick-up

  • Walk round the car before you drive off and photograph every panel, the wheels and tyres, the windscreen, the roof and the interior.
  • Take a slow video too, and capture the fuel gauge and mileage.
  • Make sure every existing mark is on the damage sheet, and ask staff to sign it if anything is missing.

Light matters

Dark car parks and rain hide scratches. If you can't see properly, ask to move the car into better light, and note the conditions.

At drop-off

  • Take the same photos and video in the same order, in daylight if you can.
  • Ask for the car to be checked with you and for a signed return report. If nobody is there, photograph the car where you left it and the key drop.

If a bill arrives later

  • Ask the hire firm for its photos, the repair invoice and the check-out and return reports.
  • Reply in writing with your dated photos and video.
  • If you paid by credit or debit card and the charge isn't justified, ask your card provider about a chargeback, and check whether your excess insurance covers it.

Keep it all together

Keep your booking, damage sheet, photos and any messages in one place for a few months after each hire, since some charges arrive weeks later.
