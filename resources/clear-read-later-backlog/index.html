How to clear a read-later backlog of hundreds of saved articles

Most read-later piles grow faster than anyone can read them. The goal isn't to read everything; it's to read the few that matter and let the rest go.

Export and count

  • Export everything from your read-later app or bookmarks first, so nothing is lost.
  • Count it and estimate the hours: at five to ten minutes an article, a thousand saves is days of reading.

Let most of it go

  • Anything older than a year that you can't remember saving can usually go.
  • News and time-sensitive pieces are often out of date; drop them.

A daily round

Take ten items a day. For each: read it now, skim it, keep it for a set time, or let it go. Ten decisions take a few minutes.

Stop the pile growing

  • Before saving, ask whether you'd read it this week.
  • Give saves an expiry date, so unread ones disappear on their own.
  • Unsubscribe from newsletters you always save but never read.

Keep the best

Keep a short list of the pieces that were genuinely worth it, and revisit them. That small list is the real value of reading later.
