Cost per mile for delivery drivers, explained

Every mile you drive for a delivery or rideshare app costs you money, even when it doesn't feel like it. Knowing your own cost per mile helps you judge whether an offer is worth taking. This is general information, not financial or tax advice.

What's in a mile

  • Gas, which changes with fuel prices and your car's efficiency.
  • Maintenance: oil changes, brakes, tyres.
  • Depreciation: your car loses value as the miles go up.
  • Insurance and other running costs, if you count part of them.

Estimate your gas cost per mile

  • Divide the price of a gallon by your car's real miles per gallon.
  • Example: $3.50 a gallon at 25 mpg is 14 cents a mile in gas.

Add the rest

  • Look at what you spent on maintenance and tyres over the last year and divide by miles driven.
  • Add a figure for depreciation if you want a fuller picture.

Use it before accepting offers

  • Multiply the offer's total miles (including the drive back) by your cost per mile.
  • Subtract that from the pay to see what you'd really keep. Only check offers when parked.
