Dog barks or howls when you leave? A day-by-day plan to build alone time

Dogs with separation anxiety panic when left. The usual approach is to practise absences so short the dog stays calm, then lengthen them very gradually.

Find the starting point

  • Film your dog after you leave (a phone or laptop camera works). Note how long it takes before the barking, pacing or whining starts.

Practise below that point

  • If your dog panics after two minutes, practise one-minute absences. The aim is that your dog never reaches panic during practice.

Build up slowly

  • Add a little time only after several calm practices in a row. Some days, go back to a shorter time.

Make leaving boring

  • Keep arrivals and departures low-key. Practise picking up keys or putting on a coat without leaving.

Get help if it is severe

If your dog hurts itself, destroys things or will not eat, talk to your vet and consider a qualified separation-anxiety trainer. This guide is general information.
