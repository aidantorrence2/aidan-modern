Scared of heights? A step-by-step ladder for balconies, bridges and viewpoints

Fear of heights is very common. Facing heights gradually, in safe places, and staying until the unease drops is the approach most often recommended.

Make a ladder

  • List situations from easiest to hardest: looking out of an upstairs window, standing on a ground-floor balcony, a low footbridge, a higher viewpoint.

Short, safe visits

  • Visit the first step for a few minutes. Only use places with railings or barriers, and stand back from the edge.

Rate before and after

  • Rate your unease from 0 to 10 at the start and end. Move up a step only when the number has dropped on a few visits.

Slow your breathing

  • Breathe in for four and out for six while you are there. If it gets too much, step back and try again later.

Get help if it is severe

If the fear seriously affects your life, a doctor or therapist can help. Never put yourself somewhere unsafe to practise. This guide is general information, not medical advice.
