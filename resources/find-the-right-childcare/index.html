How to find childcare you trust that fits your schedule

Finding childcare that you trust and that fits your working hours can feel impossible. A clear list of what you need makes the search easier.

Write down what you need

  • The days and hours you need covered, including travel time.
  • Your budget and any help you might be eligible for.
  • Must-haves, such as a short commute or flexibility on late days.

Know your options

  • Nurseries and daycare centres.
  • Registered childminders, often more flexible on hours.
  • Nannies or nanny shares.
  • Before- and after-school clubs for older children.

Check them out

  • Look up inspection reports where available.
  • Visit, ask about staff, routines and what happens if they're ill or closed.
  • Ask other parents for recommendations.

Have a back-up

Line up a back-up person or service for days when your usual care falls through.
