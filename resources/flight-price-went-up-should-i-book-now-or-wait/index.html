Flight price went up: should I book now or wait?

Flight prices change often, and nobody can reliably tell you whether a fare will drop. What you can do is decide in advance what you're willing to pay and how long you can wait. This guide is independent and not linked to any airline or booking site.

Set your limits first

  • Decide the most you're willing to pay per person, including bags and seats.
  • Decide the latest date you can book, for example before school holiday dates fill up.
  • When the price is at or under your limit, booking is a reasonable choice.

Watch the price sensibly

  • Check the same route, dates and fare type each time so you compare like with like.
  • Note the price and the date each time you check.
  • Free price alerts from search sites can save you checking every day.

Things that usually push prices up

  • School holidays, bank holidays and big events.
  • Few seats left on a route with few flights.
  • Popular departure times.

Before you book

  • Compare the total, not the headline fare: bags, seats and payment fees add up.
  • Check the change and cancellation rules.
  • Book with the airline or a protected booking where possible.
