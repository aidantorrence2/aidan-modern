Why you freeze in real conversations after years of language study, and what helps

Knowing a language and speaking it on the spot are different skills. Freezing usually means you've practised recognising words far more than producing them quickly.

Practise one real situation

  • Pick a setting you'll actually face, such as ordering food or small talk with a neighbour.
  • Learn the ten phrases that situation always needs.

Rehearse out loud

  • Say the phrases aloud daily, not just in your head.
  • Record yourself and listen back once.

Have rescue phrases ready

  • Learn how to say 'sorry, could you say that more slowly?' and 'how do you say...?'
  • These keep a conversation going when you get stuck.

Repeat the same conversation

Doing one short conversation several times builds speed faster than many different ones. Then move to the next situation.
