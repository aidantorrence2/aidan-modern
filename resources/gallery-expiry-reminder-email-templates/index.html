Gallery expiry reminder emails: three templates with an extension offer

Clients do not forget to download because they do not care. They forget because the gallery email arrived in a busy week. Three reminders, each with the download count if your host shows it, turn a resend request into a download or a small extension sale.

30 days before

Subject: Your gallery closes on [date]

Hi [names],

A gentle note that your gallery stays open until [date]. If you have not downloaded the full set yet, now is a good time: [link]. The download button is top right, and the files are full resolution.

[your name]

7 days before

Subject: One week left on your gallery

Hi [names],

Your gallery closes in a week, on [date]. I can see the full set has not been downloaded yet, so here is the link again: [link]. If you want it to stay open longer, a one-year extension is [price] and I can set it up in a minute.

[your name]

The day before

Subject: Last day for your gallery

Hi [names],

Your gallery closes tomorrow, [date]. Download the full set here: [link]. If you would rather keep it online, reply 'extend' and I will send a [price] link for another year, or [price] for a permanent archive.

Thank you again for having me,
[your name]

Notes

  • If your host shows download status, skip reminders for clients who already downloaded. A reminder to someone who has the files reads as noise.
  • Price the extension so it is a small yes, not a decision. Many photographers use the cost of a print or two.
  • Say what happens after expiry in plain words. 'Archived offline, retrievable for a fee' is honest; 'deleted' should only be said if it is true.
  • Keep every reminder in the same thread as the delivery email.
