How much fabric do I need for a quilt? Backing, binding and borders

Most fabric mistakes happen on the backing and binding, especially when you resize a pattern. A few simple calculations prevent them.

Start with the finished size

  • Decide the finished quilt width and length. Common sizes: baby, lap or throw, twin, queen and king; check a size chart if you're unsure.

Backing

  • Add about 4 inches (10 cm) to each side for quilting overhang.
  • If the quilt is wider than your fabric, you'll need to join widths, or use extra-wide backing fabric.

Binding

  • Measure the perimeter (2 × width + 2 × length), add about 10 to 15 inches for corners and joining, and divide by your usable fabric width to find how many strips to cut.

Borders and sashing

  • Add up the length of every border and sashing strip, including corners, before you buy.

Buy a little extra

Fabric can shrink when washed, and cutting mistakes happen. Many quilters add around 10% or a quarter-yard to each fabric. Write the numbers down before you go to the shop.
