How to convert oven times to air fryer times

Most packets and recipes give oven instructions only. Air fryers usually cook faster because they circulate hot air closely around the food, so you generally need a lower temperature and a shorter time. Models vary, so treat any conversion as a starting point and always check food is cooked through.

A common starting point

  • Reduce the oven temperature by about 20°C (around 25°F).
  • Reduce the time by about 20%, then check early.
  • Many packets now include air fryer instructions too; use those when they're given.

Check and shake

  • Check part-way through and shake the basket or turn the food so it browns evenly.
  • Don't overfill the basket; food cooks more evenly in a single layer.

Make sure it's cooked

  • Meat, poultry and fish must be cooked through. A food thermometer is the most reliable check.
  • Reheated food should be piping hot all the way through.
  • Follow the food-safety advice on the packet.

Keep your own notes

Write down what worked for each food in your fryer: temperature, time, and whether you'd change it. After a few weeks you'll have settings you can trust.
