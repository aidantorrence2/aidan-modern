How to find a pen pal for real letters, safely

Swapping real letters can be a lovely way to make a friend. You don't need to give out your home address to do it, and a few simple habits keep it safe and enjoyable.

Where to look

  • Established pen pal clubs and letter-writing groups.
  • Postcard-swapping projects, if you'd like to start small.
  • Hobby groups and magazines that run pen pal sections.

Protect your address

  • Consider using a PO box or a mail forwarding service instead of your home address.
  • Don't share your address until you're comfortable, and only through a trusted service.

Stay safe

  • Never send money, gift cards or bank details to a pen pal, whatever the reason.
  • Be wary of anyone who quickly turns the friendship romantic or asks for help with money.
  • Keep personal details such as your workplace and daily routine to yourself.

Keep it going

  • Agree roughly how often you'll write, so nobody feels ignored.
  • Share small everyday things; that's what makes letters fun to receive.
