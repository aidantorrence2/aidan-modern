How to find where you lose time in a sim racing lap

Knowing that you're slow doesn't help much; knowing where you're slow does. You can find it with simple notes, sector times, or telemetry if you have it. This guide is independent and not affiliated with any sim or coaching service.

Use sector times

  • Most sims show sector or split times; note which sector is furthest from your best.
  • Within that sector, pick the slowest corner.

Compare with a reference

  • Watch a replay of a faster driver, or a reference lap, through the same corner.
  • Note their brake point, their line and when they get back on the throttle.

Watch your own replay

  • Look for lifts on exit, which usually mean an early turn-in or too much speed in.
  • Look for sliding or running wide, which usually mean too much throttle too early.

If you have telemetry

  • Compare speed traces: where does the faster lap carry more speed?
  • Compare brake and throttle traces at the corner you picked.
  • Change one thing and run the comparison again.
