How to learn tai chi at home as a complete beginner

Tai chi is a gentle practice of slow, flowing movements. You can start at home with a little space and a few minutes a day. This guide is general information, not medical advice; if you have a health condition, balance problems or have had falls, check with your doctor first.

Set up

  • Wear loose, comfortable clothes and flat shoes, or bare feet on a non-slip floor.
  • Clear a small space, and practise near a sturdy chair or wall you can hold if you need to.

Start with the basics

  • Stand with feet about shoulder-width apart, knees soft, shoulders relaxed.
  • Breathe slowly and naturally.
  • Practise shifting your weight slowly from one foot to the other.

Learn one move at a time

  • Pick one move and practise it slowly until it feels natural before adding the next.
  • Go slower than the video if you need to; pause and repeat.
  • Small movements are fine; don't force your range.

Keep it up

  • Ten minutes a day is better than an hour once a week.
  • Practise at the same time each day, such as after breakfast.
  • Keep a simple note of the moves you've learned.

Find a class if you can

Community centres, libraries and local groups sometimes run low-cost tai chi classes, where a teacher can check your form.
