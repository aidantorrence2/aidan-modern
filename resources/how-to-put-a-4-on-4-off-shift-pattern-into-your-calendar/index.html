How to put a 4 on 4 off shift pattern into your phone calendar

A 4 on 4 off pattern repeats every 8 days, so it doesn't line up with weeks or months. Most phone calendars can still handle it with a custom repeat. Always check against your employer's official rota.

Work out the cycle

  • 4 on 4 off repeats every 8 days.
  • If your pattern mixes days and nights, write out one full cycle, day by day, before you start.

Add it to your calendar

  • Create an event for your first working day in the cycle.
  • Set it to repeat every 8 days (a custom repeat).
  • Do the same for each of the other three working days, starting on the right date.

For day and night rotations

  • Use separate events, with different colours, for day shifts and night shifts.
  • Each may need its own repeat interval if the full cycle is longer.

Keep it accurate

  • Add swaps, overtime and leave as one-off changes.
  • Check a few weeks ahead against your official rota now and then.

Share it

Share the calendar with your partner or family so they can see when you're working without asking.
