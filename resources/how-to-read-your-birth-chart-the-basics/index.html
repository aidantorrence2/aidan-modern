How to read your birth chart: the basics

A birth chart can look like a wheel of confusing symbols. It's easier when you learn it in layers. Astrology is a hobby and a tool for reflection; this guide makes no predictions.

The four building blocks

  • Planets: the Sun, Moon and planets, each traditionally linked with a different area of life.
  • Signs: the twelve zodiac signs, describing the style in which each planet is said to express itself.
  • Houses: twelve sections of the chart, each linked with areas such as home, work or relationships.
  • Aspects: the angles between planets, shown as lines across the middle of the chart.

Start with three placements

  • Your Sun sign, Moon sign and rising sign (Ascendant) are the usual starting points.
  • Read about each one before adding anything else.

Then add houses

  • Look at which house each planet sits in.
  • Learn the houses one or two at a time rather than all twelve at once.

Pick one approach

  • Modern Western, traditional and Vedic astrology interpret charts differently, which is why sources can disagree.
  • Choose one approach while you learn so the pieces fit together.

Keep it in perspective

Enjoy astrology as a hobby and for reflection. Don't use it to make decisions about health, money or relationships.
