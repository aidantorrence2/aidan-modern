How to share school pickups and kids' activities with your partner (so it's not all in one head)

In many households one parent ends up holding the whole family schedule in their head. A short weekly check-in and a clear 'who's doing it' for each job makes things fairer and means fewer missed pickups.

Do a 10-minute Sunday check-in

  • Go through the coming week together: school, clubs, appointments, work travel.
  • Give every pickup and drop-off a named person, not 'one of us'.

Keep one shared view

  • Use one place both of you actually look at, whether a shared calendar, an app or a whiteboard.
  • Write the person's name on each item.

Make changes visible

  • If plans change, update the shared view straight away and tell the other person.
  • Agree how to ask for a swap, so it's easy rather than awkward.

Share the planning, not just the driving

Take turns being the one who checks the week ahead, so the mental load doesn't always sit with the same person.
