How to stop creeping over the speed limit without noticing

Most speeding tickets aren't for racing; they're for drifting a few mph over without noticing, often in 20mph or 30mph zones. A few habits make it much easier to stay under. This is general information, not legal advice.

Habits that help

  • Use a lower gear in 20mph zones, which makes it harder to drift up.
  • If your car has a speed limiter or cruise control, use it where it's safe and legal, and still watch the road and signs.
  • Leave a few minutes earlier so you're not tempted to make up time.

Know where you creep

  • Most people creep on the same few roads: long straight 30s, downhill stretches, and roads where the limit drops.
  • Note those spots on your regular routes and check your speed as you approach them.

Road signs come first

Sat-navs and apps sometimes show the wrong limit. Always follow the road signs, and look for repeater signs after junctions.

Keep track of points (UK)

  • Speeding points usually stay on your licence for 4 years from the offence date.
  • If you reach 12 or more points within 3 years, you can be banned from driving.
  • If you passed your test in the last 2 years, 6 points can mean your licence is revoked.
  • Check your record on GOV.UK's 'View or share your driving licence information' service.

Stay safe

Never touch your phone while driving. Set any app or sat-nav up before you set off, and use a proper mount.
