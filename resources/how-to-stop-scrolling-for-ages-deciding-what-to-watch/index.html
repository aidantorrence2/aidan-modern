How to stop scrolling for ages deciding what to watch

With several streaming services, choosing what to watch can take longer than the show. A bit of preparation and a simple rule for the evening makes it much quicker.

Keep one shared list

  • Whenever someone recommends a show or film, add it to one list straight away, whichever service it's on.
  • Note which service it's on and roughly how long it is.

Decide the limits first

  • Agree how much time you have tonight before you start looking.
  • Pick the mood: something light, gripping, or familiar.

Use a quick picking rule

  • Each person suggests up to three options from the list.
  • Each person can veto one.
  • Choose from what's left, or take turns choosing on different nights.

Keep track of what you've watched

  • Mark shows as finished, abandoned or in progress, so you don't revisit the same options every night.
  • Set a time limit for choosing, such as five minutes; if nothing's chosen, go back to something you're halfway through.
