How to stop the clean laundry pile on the chair

For many people, washing isn't the problem: putting it away is. Clean clothes sit in baskets or on a chair for days. A few small changes can stop the pile from building.

Make putting away part of the load

  • Decide a load isn't done until it's put away, not just dry.
  • Fold straight out of the dryer or off the line, while it's still in one place.

Keep it small

  • Do one load at a time, rather than a whole weekend's worth at once.
  • Spend 10 minutes a day putting away whatever is clean.
  • Put on a podcast or music while you fold.

Make it easier

  • Fold less: underwear, socks and gym clothes can go straight into a drawer or bin.
  • Hang more: shirts and dresses can go on hangers straight from the line or dryer.
  • Give everyone in the household their own basket to take to their room.

Find the sticking point

Notice where laundry gets stuck: in the washer, the dryer, the basket or the chair. Fix that one step first.
