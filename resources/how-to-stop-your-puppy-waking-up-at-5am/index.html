How to stop your puppy or dog waking you up at 5am

Early waking usually has a reason: needing the toilet, light, noise, hunger, or learning that whining gets attention. Changing the evening and the sleeping space, then moving the morning slowly, often helps. Check with your vet first if early waking is sudden, or your dog seems unwell, or is older.

Look at the evening

  • Give a last toilet trip as late as is practical.
  • Make sure your dog has had enough exercise and play during the day.
  • Try moving dinner a little later, if your vet agrees.

Change the sleeping space

  • Block early light with blackout curtains or covering a crate (leaving airflow).
  • Use soft background noise to mask early sounds like birds or traffic.

Respond carefully in the morning

  • If your puppy needs the toilet, take it out calmly and quietly, then straight back to bed.
  • Avoid starting the day, playing or feeding as soon as your dog whines.
  • Gradually wait a few minutes longer before getting up, moving wake-up later over days or weeks.

Be patient

Young puppies often can't hold on through the night yet; this usually improves as they grow.
