Which skincare product is breaking you out? Change one thing at a time

When you change several products at once, you can never tell which one helped or caused a breakout. The fix is slow but reliable: one change at a time, with notes and photos so you are not relying on memory.

Strip back to a simple base

  • Start from a basic routine: a gentle cleanser, a plain moisturiser and sunscreen in the day.
  • Pause the extras for now, especially if several were added recently.
  • Stay on the simple base for a couple of weeks so you have a starting point.

Add or change one product at a time

  • Introduce one new product, and only one, at a time.
  • Give it several weeks before judging, unless it stings, burns or causes a reaction, in which case stop.
  • Patch test new products on a small area first.

Keep a short log

  • For each product, write the start date and the stop date.
  • Rate your skin each morning on a simple scale, from clear to bad.
  • Take a photo once a week in the same spot, same light and same angle.
  • Note other things that might matter: sleep, stress, your cycle, new makeup, a new pillowcase or phone habits.

Read the results

After each change, compare the weeks before and after. If things improved, keep it. If nothing changed, you may not need it. If things got worse, drop it and wait for your skin to settle before the next change.

When to get help

If acne is painful, leaves marks or scars, or does not improve, see a doctor or dermatologist. Bring your log and photos; they make the appointment more useful. This guide is not medical advice.
