Inherited a stamp collection? Where to start

Most inherited collections are mostly common stamps, with a few that deserve a closer look. A calm, careful sort helps you find them.

Handle with care

  • Don't peel stamps off album pages or covers, and don't clean them. Handle them by the edges, ideally with stamp tongs.

Sort roughly first

  • Group albums and loose stamps by country, then by rough era (Victorian, early 1900s, modern).

Note what stands out

  • Older stamps in good condition, full sheets, unused stamps with gum, covers (envelopes) with interesting postmarks, and anything with certificates.

Make an inventory

  • List what's in each album, with photos. It helps family decide, and helps any expert you ask.

Get an expert view

A local stamp society, a reputable dealer or an auction house can tell you what's worth more attention. Catalogue prices are often much higher than what stamps sell for, so get a realistic opinion before selling.
