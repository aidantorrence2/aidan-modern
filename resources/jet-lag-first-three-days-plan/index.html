Jet lag after a long-haul flight: a day-by-day plan for the first three days

Jet lag usually eases faster when you line up light, sleep and meals with your new time zone. This is general information, not medical advice.

Before you fly

  • Move bedtime an hour or so towards your destination's time for a couple of nights, if you can.

On the plane

  • Set your watch to local time and sleep or stay awake to match it as best you can.

Day one

  • Get outdoor daylight, stay up until a normal local bedtime, and keep any nap short.

Eat and drink on local time

  • Have meals at local times and stop caffeine in the afternoon.

Days two and three

Keep the same wake time each morning. If you have a health condition or take medication, ask a pharmacist before using sleep aids.
