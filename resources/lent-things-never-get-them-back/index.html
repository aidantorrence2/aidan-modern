Lend things and never get them back? A simple lending list

Most lent things don't come back because both people forget. A simple record and a return date fix most of it.

Write every loan down

  • Note the item, who has it and the date. A note on your phone is enough.

Agree a return date

  • Say when you'd like it back as you hand it over. It makes asking later much easier.

Put your name on things

  • A name label inside books and on tools helps them find their way home.

Check the list monthly

  • Look over what's out once a month and send a friendly reminder for anything overdue.

Keep a no-lend shelf

Some things are too precious to lend. Decide which, and say so kindly.
