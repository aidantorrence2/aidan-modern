Lost the receipt for something still under warranty? What to do

Losing a receipt doesn't always mean losing your warranty. Many shops and makers accept other proof of purchase. This guide is general information, not legal advice; rules differ by country and by shop.

Look for other proof

  • A bank or card statement showing the shop, date and amount.
  • The order confirmation email, or your account's order history if you bought online.
  • A registration confirmation if you registered the product with the maker.
  • A delivery note or gift receipt.

Ask the shop

  • Many shops can look up a purchase or reprint a receipt if you give them the date, the card used and roughly the amount.
  • Some keep records only for a limited time, so ask soon.

Contact the maker

  • The manufacturer's warranty may be separate from the shop's returns policy.
  • Give them the serial number and any proof you have; some will accept a statement plus the serial number.

Check your rights

If the item is faulty, you may have legal rights separate from any warranty. Check your country's consumer advice service for what applies.

Next time

Photograph the receipt on the day you buy, and keep it with the warranty length and serial number.
