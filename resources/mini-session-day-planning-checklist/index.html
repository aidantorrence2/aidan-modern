Mini session day planning checklist: slots, deposits, waitlist, rain plan

Mini days make or lose money in the planning week, not on the day. This is the checklist I use before announcing one. Copy it into your own notes and fill it in once; most of it carries over to the next date.

Decide before you announce

  • Date, location, and a backup date at the same location, booked now, not after the forecast turns.
  • Slot length and count: fifteen minutes with a five-minute buffer gives three families an hour; twelve slots is a full afternoon of light in autumn.
  • Price, deposit and what is included: say the image count and the delivery window in the first message, not the third.
  • Deposit rule in one sentence: holds the slot, applied to the balance, moved to the backup date for weather, refunded only if you cancel.
  • Cut-off: when booking closes and when the balance is due, so the day before is not a payment chase.

Filling it

  • One page or post with the slots visible. Families book faster when they can see which times are left.
  • Take the deposit at booking; a slot without money is a maybe. Maybes are what no-shows are made of.
  • Open a waitlist the moment the last slot goes. A cancellation offered to the next name on the list is filled within the hour; a cancellation announced publicly is filled by someone you have to onboard from scratch.
  • Announce once, remind twice: a week out and two days out, with the forecast and the rain plan in the second.

What each family gets after booking

Subject: Your mini session: [date], [time]

Hi [name],

You are booked for [time] on [date] at [location]: [pin or link]. Please arrive ten minutes early; we start on the minute so every family gets their full slot.

What to wear: [two lines, your palette]. What to bring: [props, snacks, a change of clothes for toddlers].

Rain plan: if the forecast on the morning shows [your rule, e.g. more than 60 percent chance of rain at your time], we move to [backup date] at the same time and your deposit moves with you. I will message by [time] that morning.

Gallery: [n] images within [n] days, with a link to choose extras.

[your name]

On the day

  • Arrive an hour early, walk the light, choose two spots so a crying toddler can be moved, not stopped.
  • Keep a printed list by time with phone numbers. Tick as families arrive; text the next family when you are running five minutes behind so they do not stack up.
  • Shoot the first frame on the minute. Fifteen minutes is enough when you are not spending five of them deciding where to stand.
  • Hold the last slot for the waitlist no-show rule: if a family is ten minutes late with no message, text the next waitlisted family for the final slot.

After

  • Deliver on the day you promised, all galleries at once, with the extras link live.
  • Before galleries go out, send the next date to this list first. A family that just loved their photos books the spring minis in the same message.
  • Write down what broke: the slot that ran long, the spot that lost light, the deposit question you answered six times. Fix the page, not your memory.
