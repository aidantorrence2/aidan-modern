How to photograph the moon rising with your phone

The moon is bright and small in the frame, which can confuse phone cameras. A few tweaks make a big difference.

Timing

  • Shoot close to moonrise, around sunset on a full moon night, when the sky still has some light.
  • That balances the bright moon with the landscape, so both show detail.

Camera settings

  • Tap the moon on your screen to focus on it.
  • Slide the exposure down until the moon shows detail rather than a white blob.
  • Use your phone's zoom lens if it has one; heavy digital zoom adds blur.

Keep it steady

  • Rest the phone on something solid or use a small tripod.
  • Use the timer so tapping the button doesn't shake the phone.

Make it interesting

Include something in the foreground, like a skyline, trees or the sea, to show scale and place.
