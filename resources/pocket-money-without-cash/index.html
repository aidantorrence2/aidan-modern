Paying pocket money when you never carry cash

Plenty of parents want to give pocket money but rarely have coins on them. A simple written balance solves most of it.

Keep a running balance

  • One line per child: what they've been given, what they've spent, and what's left.
  • Paper, a notes app or a spreadsheet all work. What matters is that it's kept up to date.

Pick a set day

  • Add pocket money on the same day each week. It's easier to remember and children learn to wait for it.

Note gifts and IOUs

  • Add birthday money and gifts, and note any money you lend or borrow, so nothing is forgotten.

Let them see it

  • Children learn more about money when they can check their own balance and watch it grow or shrink.

Cash out regularly

Hand over real money from time to time, or when they want to buy something, and take it off the balance. Seeing cash change hands keeps it real.
