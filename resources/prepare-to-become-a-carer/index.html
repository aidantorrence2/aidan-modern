How to prepare when you're about to become a carer

Becoming a carer often happens quickly. A few early steps can make the change easier for you and the person you're caring for.

Understand their needs

  • Talk with the person you'll care for about what help they want.
  • Ask their doctor or care team what to expect.

Get organised

  • Keep a list of medications, appointments and important contacts.
  • Find out where key documents are kept.

Find out what help is available

  • Ask your local council or health service about a carer's assessment or support.
  • Check whether financial support for carers applies to you.
  • Look up local carers' groups and charities.

Look after yourself too

Plan regular breaks, and tell your own doctor you're a carer. You'll care better if you're supported.
