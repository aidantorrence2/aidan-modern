Puppy still peeing inside? A three-day log that shows when to go out

Most toilet accidents follow a pattern: after waking, eating, playing or drinking. A short log shows you when to go out.

Log three days

  • Note the time of every wee and poo, indoors or out, and what happened just before.

Find the pattern

  • Look for clusters after sleep, meals, play or drinks.

Go out just before

  • Take your puppy out just before those times, and reward the moment they go outside.

Clean with an enzyme cleaner

  • It removes the smell that draws your puppy back to the same spot.

See the vet if

an adult dog that was clean starts having accidents, or your dog seems to need to go much more often.
