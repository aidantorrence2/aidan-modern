Moving into a rental? The 20-minute photo walk-through that protects your deposit

Deposit disputes often come down to what condition the place was in on day one. A dated record made at move-in is your best evidence.

Photograph every room

  • Walls, floors, windows, appliances, inside cupboards and the bathroom. Take close-ups of any marks or damage.

Note it in writing

  • List anything broken, stained or worn, room by room.

Send it the same week

  • Email your photos and notes to your landlord or agent, so there's a dated copy they've received.

Repeat at move-out

  • Take the same photos from the same spots when you leave.

Check your deposit is protected

In many places deposits must be held in a protection scheme. If there's a dispute, check the scheme's free resolution service.
