How to return to work after a long gap

A career gap is common and increasingly understood by employers. Preparing a short, honest explanation and refreshing a few skills makes the return easier.

Explain the gap simply

One or two sentences is enough, such as 'I took time out to care for a family member and I'm now ready to return.'

Refresh what's changed

  • Look at recent job adverts in your field and note new tools or skills they mention.
  • Pick one or two to brush up on with a short course.

Look for returner routes

Some employers run returnship programmes or part-time starts for people coming back after a break.

Reconnect

Let former colleagues know you're looking. Many returners find their first role through someone they used to work with.
