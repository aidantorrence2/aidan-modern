Second shooter call sheet template for weddings

A second shooter who shows up with the right lens at the right door is the result of one page sent the week before, not a text at midnight. Copy this and fill it in for every job.

The call sheet

JOB: [couple's names], [date]
LEAD: [your name], [phone]
SECOND: [name], [phone]

CALL TIME: [time] at [address or pin]
WRAP: [time, or 'after first dance']

TIMELINE (second's role in brackets)
[time]  Getting ready, Partner B's suite  [cover Partner B; details, flat lays]
[time]  First look / ceremony arrivals    [guests arriving, wide of venue]
[time]  Ceremony                           [opposite angle to lead; reactions, back rows]
[time]  Family formals                     [gather next group from the list; grab candids]
[time]  Couple portraits                   [reception room empty, details, cocktail hour candids]
[time]  Reception                          [reactions during speeches; second angle on first dance]

MUST-HAVES FOR THE SECOND
- [e.g. grandmother arriving, groom's reaction, the band]

GEAR
Bring: [bodies, lenses, flash]. Cards: shoot to two cards, do not format. Hand over all cards at wrap.

FILES
Deliver: [raw cards / card copy / upload link] by [date]. No sharing or posting of any image; credit terms: [e.g. may share after the couple's gallery goes live, with credit to lead].

RATE AND PAYMENT
[amount] for [hours], overtime [rate]. Paid by [method] within [days] of delivery. Travel: [included / per mile].

DRESS: [dark, no logos]. PARKING: [note]. MEALS: [vendor meal confirmed / bring your own].

Send it with

  • The approved family formals list, so they can gather the next group while you shoot.
  • A pin for every location, not just an address. Venues have the wrong front door.
  • Your phone number and the planner's, with a note on which one to call for what.

After the job

  • Confirm the cards or files arrived the same day, in writing.
  • Pay on the day you said. Reliability is why the good ones answer your next message first.
  • Keep a note of who was free, who was great and what they charged. Next season's list builds itself.
