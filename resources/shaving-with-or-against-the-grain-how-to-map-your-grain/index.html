Shaving with or against the grain, and how to map your grain

The grain is the direction your hair grows. Shaving with it is gentler on the skin; shaving against it gives a closer shave but is more likely to cause irritation and ingrown hairs for many people.

Find your grain

  • Let your stubble grow for a day or two.
  • Run your fingers over each area: the direction that feels smooth is with the grain, and the direction that feels rough is against it.
  • On the neck the direction often changes, sometimes growing sideways or upwards.

Draw a simple map

  • Sketch your face and neck and add arrows for each area.
  • Note any spots where the hair swirls or changes direction.

Use the map when you shave

  • Do your first pass with the grain everywhere.
  • If you want a closer shave, try a second pass across the grain rather than against it, and see how your skin reacts.

Watch the results

Note how your skin looks the next day. If bumps appear in the same place, check your map for that area and try a gentler approach there.
