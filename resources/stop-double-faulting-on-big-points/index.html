Stop double faulting on big points: a 15-minute second-serve routine

Most club players double fault on big points for the same few reasons: the toss drifts, the rhythm speeds up under pressure, and the second serve has no spin to bring it down.

Fix the toss first

  • Toss without hitting: catch it ten times. It should land in front and slightly to the side of your front foot.
  • An inconsistent toss causes most other serve problems.

Slow the rhythm

  • Use the same routine every time: bounce the ball, breathe out, then toss.
  • Under pressure people rush; a fixed routine keeps the tempo.

Spin on the second serve

  • A second serve needs topspin or slice so it drops into the box. Practise brushing up the back of the ball rather than hitting through it.

Practise under pressure

  • In practice, serve as if it were 30-40: one first serve, then a second serve that must go in.
  • Count how many second serves land out of twenty, and track it each week.

Get it filmed

Film your serve from the side and behind. A coach can spot a fault in minutes that is hard to feel yourself.
