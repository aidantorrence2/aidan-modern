Tablet meltdowns when screen time ends: a countdown and handover routine

Screens are hard to stop because they're absorbing. A predictable ending, with warnings and something to move to, usually makes it easier.

Agree the time first

  • Tell your child how long they have before they start.

Give two warnings

  • For example, ten minutes and two minutes before the end. A visual timer helps younger children.

Plan the next thing

  • Agree in advance what happens next, like a snack, a game or going outside.

Stay calm and consistent

  • Use the same routine every day, even when there's protest.

Note what makes it harder

Tiredness, hunger or certain games can make endings harder. If you're worried, talk to your health visitor or doctor.
