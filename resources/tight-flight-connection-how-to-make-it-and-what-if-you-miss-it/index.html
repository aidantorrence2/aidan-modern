Tight flight connection? How to make it, and what to do if you miss it

A short connection can be fine or very stressful depending on the airport, terminals and checks involved. A little preparation helps a lot. This is general information, not legal advice; check your airline's and ticket's terms.

Before you fly

  • Check whether both flights are on one booking or separate tickets; airlines usually rebook missed connections on one booking, but not on separate tickets.
  • Find out whether you'll change terminals, pass security again, go through passport control or collect bags.
  • Look at a map of the connecting airport.

On the plane

  • Tell the crew you have a tight connection; sometimes they can help.
  • Sit near the front if you can, and keep your bag and documents ready.

After landing

  • Follow the 'connections' or 'transfers' signs.
  • Check the departures screens for your gate, as it may have changed.

If you miss it

  • On one booking, go to the airline's transfer or service desk for rebooking.
  • On separate tickets, contact the airline for your next flight as soon as possible and check what your ticket allows.
  • Keep receipts and boarding passes in case you can claim later.
