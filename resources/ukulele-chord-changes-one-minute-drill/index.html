Ukulele chord changes too slow? A one-minute daily drill

Most beginners get stuck on one or two chord changes. Drilling just those, for a minute a day, speeds them up faster than playing whole songs.

Pick one pair

  • For example C to Am, or G to C. Just the one that slows you down.

Count for a minute

  • Change back and forth without strumming. Count clean changes and write the number down.

Move fingers together

  • Lift and place your fingers as a group, keeping them close to the strings.

Strum slowly

  • Use a simple down strum at a slow beat before trying patterns like the island strum.

Short sessions

Ten minutes is plenty while your fingertips toughen up. Stop if anything hurts.
