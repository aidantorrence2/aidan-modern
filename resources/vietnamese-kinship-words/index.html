Vietnamese kinship words: what to call your relatives

Vietnamese uses family words instead of 'you' and 'I', so knowing them makes conversations with relatives much easier. Usage varies by region and family, so check with yours.

Parents and grandparents

  • Bố or Ba: Dad (Bố is more common in the north, Ba in the south).
  • Mẹ or Má: Mum (Mẹ in the north, Má often in the south).
  • Ông nội, Bà nội: your father's father and mother.
  • Ông ngoại, Bà ngoại: your mother's father and mother.

Brothers and sisters

  • Anh: older brother, or an older man of your generation.
  • Chị: older sister, or an older woman of your generation.
  • Em: a younger brother or sister.

Aunts and uncles

  • Chú: your father's younger brother.
  • Bác: an older sibling of your parent (exact use varies by region).
  • Cô: your father's sister.
  • Cậu: your mother's brother.
  • Dì: your mother's sister.

Talking about yourself

  • With your parents, call yourself con.
  • With your grandparents, call yourself cháu.
  • With older siblings, call yourself em.
