Wedding planning when you don't know where to start: the first five decisions

Wedding planning feels huge because everything seems urgent at once. In reality, a few early decisions shape all the rest, and most things can wait.

The first five decisions

  • Your total budget, and who is contributing.
  • Roughly how many guests.
  • The season or rough date.
  • Your top three priorities (for example food, photos and music).
  • The general area or type of venue.

Split the budget by priorities

  • Put more of the budget into your top three priorities, and less into the things you care least about.
  • Keep a buffer of around 10% for surprises.
  • Update the budget as real quotes come in.

Book the big things first

Venue and date usually come first, then the suppliers who get booked up early in your area. Everything else can follow later.

Take it a few steps at a time

Choose two or three tasks a week and ignore the rest until next week. A long checklist is useful to keep, but not to look at every day.

Share the load

Decide together who handles what, and keep one shared list so nothing gets lost in messages.
