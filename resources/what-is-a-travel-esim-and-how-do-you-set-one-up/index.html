What is a travel eSIM, and how do you set one up?

An eSIM is a SIM built into your phone that you can download instead of inserting a plastic card. A travel eSIM gives you mobile data abroad, often for less than roaming. Steps vary by phone, so follow the provider's instructions too.

Check your phone first

  • Your phone needs to support eSIM; many recent phones do. Check your phone's settings or the maker's website.
  • Your phone usually needs to be unlocked, meaning not tied to one network.

Before you travel

  • Buy the eSIM for your destination and number of days.
  • Install it while you're on Wi-Fi at home, usually by scanning a QR code or using the provider's app.
  • Don't delete it after installing; you can usually switch it on when you arrive.

When you arrive

  • Set the travel eSIM as your mobile data line.
  • Turn off data roaming on your home SIM so it doesn't use data abroad.
  • You can usually keep your home SIM on for calls and texts if you want, but check what those cost.

After the trip

Switch your mobile data back to your home SIM, and remove the travel eSIM when its plan has ended if you won't use it again.
