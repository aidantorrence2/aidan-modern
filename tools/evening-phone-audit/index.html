Evening phone audit

Check your phone's own screen-time numbers, say what you would rather be doing, and see what your evenings are actually going to. Then get one evening rule and a printable phone parking plan.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; your answers are saved only on this device.

Where to find your numbers (30 seconds)

iPhone

  1. Open Settings, then Screen Time, then See All App & Website Activity.
  2. Tap Day, then swipe the chart back to a recent ordinary weekday. The bar chart shows use hour by hour.
  3. Add up the bars from when you get home until bed. The apps list below the chart shows where the time went.

Android

  1. Open Settings, then Digital Wellbeing & parental controls (on Samsung: Digital Wellbeing and parental controls).
  2. Tap the chart or Dashboard to see screen time per day and per app. Tap a day to look back.
  3. If your phone does not show hours of the day, enter your daily total below and pick roughly how much of it happens in the evening.
