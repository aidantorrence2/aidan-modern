New-city friendship planner

One-off events with strangers rarely turn into friends. Seeing the same people again and again usually does. Tell the planner what you like and when you are free, and it builds a 4-week plan around one weekly thing, plus a place to keep the names you learn.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; your answers are saved only on this device.
