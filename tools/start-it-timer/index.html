Start-it timer

You do not need motivation for the whole task. You need the first two minutes. Name the task, pick a first action small enough to need no decisions, then run ten focused minutes and log what got done.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; your answers are saved only on this device.
