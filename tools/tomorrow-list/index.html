The tomorrow list

Five minutes to get tomorrow out of your head and onto paper. Write everything down, sort each item into tomorrow, someday or not mine to fix, then print the list and leave it by the bed. It is a wind-down habit, not a treatment.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; your answers are saved only on this device.

This is a habit for winding down, not a treatment for sleep problems or anxiety. If you have trouble sleeping most nights for weeks, or it is affecting your days, talk to a doctor or a qualified sleep professional. If you are in crisis, in the US call or text 988.
